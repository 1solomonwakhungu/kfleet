# Prioritized 90-Minute Application Sequence

**Objective:** Maximize submission readiness without rushing user-only decisions or submitting anything automatically.  
**Order rationale:** Lemon.io exposes the most complete form and therefore gives the fastest deterministic progress. Gun.io and Turing unlock their unknown conditional steps next. Codementor is a short shared-account/profile inventory. Contra is last because four work samples and identity/wallet setup make it the largest material gap.

## Minute 0-10: Create one verified fact sheet

- Confirm legal name, application email, phone, residence, LinkedIn/GitHub URLs, education, employer dates, commercial start year, years by skill, availability, start readiness, and rate boundaries.
- Open the existing profile-copy files only as drafts. Strike any metric that cannot be substantiated or safely disclosed.
- Locate/export a current PDF CV. Do not proceed with invented dates or placeholders.

**Checkpoint at 10:** one internally consistent fact sheet and a current PDF CV, or defer forms requiring the missing facts.

## Minute 10-30: Lemon.io public form

- Choose full-time, part-time, or just checking based on actual 20+ hour availability.
- Set personally approved minimum and desired hourly rates.
- Select specialization and seniority, commercial start year, and 1-6 current core technologies with truthful recent years.
- Provide LinkedIn, CV link, or accepted CV upload; choose the truthful English-readiness answer.
- Enter legal name, email, and residence.
- **Stop before Register.** Review terms/privacy and every value; only Solomon may create the account.

**Checkpoint at 30:** Lemon.io is either ready for Solomon's Register action or has an exact unresolved-field list.

## Minute 30-45: Gun.io account gate and profile inventory

- Choose **To Find Work**, confirm talent-profile intent, and enter the selected application email.
- Solomon personally requests and opens the emailed completion link.
- Complete credentials, legal name, phone, truthful attribution, and terms only after review.
- Before filling the talent profile, record every actual field, required marker, upload constraint, rate/availability control, and consent shown.

**Checkpoint at 45:** account gate completed by Solomon and authenticated profile requirements inventoried. Do not infer resume/GitHub requirements.

## Minute 45-65: Turing account gate and profile skeleton

- Choose email or Google; Solomon completes verification.
- Upload the verified PDF CV or deliberately use Skip. Review every parsed value.
- Fill legal/contact and education facts, preferred role, skills with exact years, languages, and work history from the fact sheet.
- Treat GitHub and other professional links as optional. Do not claim a universal test path.
- **Stop before Submit Profile** until conditional summary/publication fields and all parsed facts are reviewed.

**Checkpoint at 65:** authenticated profile is accurately staged or its remaining conditional gaps are recorded.

## Minute 65-77: Codementor mentor entry

- Use `/mentor/apply` and choose email or a personally approved social option.
- Solomon creates the Arc-hosted shared account and inventories the auth-gated mentor fields.
- Prepare photo, headline, short bio, expertise entries with 1-3 evidence-rich sentences, work/project history, languages, and professional links where the live form requests them.
- Do not assume rate/calendar/photo/GitHub are mandatory initial fields. If Persona verification is requested, Solomon handles the government ID and selfie.

**Checkpoint at 77:** exact gated mentor requirements are known and verified copy is mapped to them.

## Minute 77-90: Contra gap closure, not purchase

- Create or verify the fourth genuine work-sample outline.
- Select rights-cleared cover/media for all four works and the profile; prepare one-liner, hourly rate, and social/portfolio URL.
- If enough material exists, Solomon may create the email/Google account and complete the code.
- Use the Free path. Do not buy Pro as part of this sequence.
- Do not start Persona or wallet setup unless Solomon has time to review ID issuer, privacy terms, payout details, and tax implications. Do not submit a job application in this block.

**Checkpoint at 90:** Contra has four publishable work packets or a precise asset list; no purchase or rushed identity/payment action has occurred.

## End-of-session record

For each platform, record one of: `Ready for user submit`, `Authenticated draft`, or `Blocked by [exact fact/material]`. Record the current URL and time. Never call a profile or application submitted unless the platform confirms it after Solomon's own action.
